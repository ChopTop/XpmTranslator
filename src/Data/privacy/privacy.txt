Introduction
Xiaomi PC Manager is software pre-installed on Xiaomi laptops. It is provided by Xiaomi Inc. and its affiliates (hereinafter "Xiaomi" or "we") to Xiaomi laptop users and offers on-device services and device connectivity services. On-device services include hardware and software information, FAQ search, driver repair and updates, disk cleanup, system acceleration, AI screen brightness adjustment, AI smart scheduling, AI file search, feedback, after-sales service, push notifications and more. Device connectivity services include transferring files, media, apps, information and other content between devices, as well as mutual control and collaboration between devices. The Xiaomi PC Manager services described in this privacy policy may differ depending on your device model, system version, app version and other factors; the services actually supported by the Xiaomi PC Manager you use shall prevail.

We take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy is Xiaomi's statement and commitment regarding privacy for Xiaomi PC Manager and is an important part of the Xiaomi Privacy Policy. Where special provisions for Xiaomi PC Manager are inconsistent with the Xiaomi Privacy Policy, this privacy policy shall prevail; for matters not covered by this privacy policy, the Xiaomi Privacy Policy shall prevail. In particular, regarding "protection of minors' information, security measures and cross-border transfers", we recommend that you read the Xiaomi Privacy Policy (https://privacy.mi.com/all/zh_CN/) carefully.

This privacy policy was written with your needs fully in mind. It is essential that you fully understand our practices for collecting and using personal information, and that you ultimately have control over the personal information you provide to us. This privacy policy sets out how we collect, use, disclose, process and store the information you provide when using Xiaomi PC Manager. In this privacy policy, "personal information" means data that can identify a specific individual, either on its own or in combination with other information. We will use such information in strict compliance with this privacy policy.

Finally, we want to bring our users the best possible experience. If you have any questions about the personal information processing practices described in this privacy policy, please contact us at https://privacy.mi.com/support so that we can address your specific needs.

1. What information we collect and how we use it
We follow the principles of legitimacy, lawfulness, transparency and necessity, and, for the purposes described in this privacy policy, collect and use the personal information that you actively provide or that is generated while you use our products and/or services. If we intend to use your personal information for purposes not stated in this privacy policy, or to use personal information collected for a specific purpose for another purpose, we will inform you in a reasonable manner and ask for your consent again before such use.

Please note that sensitive personal information is highlighted in "bold" as a special reminder, and we ask you to pay particular attention to it when reading. Before you provide any sensitive personal information to us, you have carefully considered and hereby confirm that providing it is appropriate, and you hereby agree that your sensitive personal information may be processed for the purposes and in the manner described in this policy.

(1) Cases in which you must authorize us to collect and use your personal information
We collect personal information in order to provide products and/or services to you and to ensure that we comply with applicable laws, regulations and other normative documents. You may choose whether to provide this information, but in most cases, if you do not provide it, we may be unable to provide the corresponding service or respond to the problems you encounter. The features include:

1. On-device services
(1) My PC

You can sign in with your Xiaomi Account. After sign-in, your Xiaomi Account ID, nickname and avatar are shown on the PC Manager home page; this information is retrieved from the Xiaomi Account server and displayed. In "My PC" you can view the hardware configuration of this device, change system settings, look up driver downloads and view the shortcut guide and user manual. For this purpose we collect your hardware configuration information (including model, product serial number, motherboard, CPU, graphics card, operating system, hard disk, memory, display, sound card and network adapter configuration); this data is kept only locally on the device. To provide display settings adjustment, when you change display settings we need to collect your product serial number in order to recommend the best screen color calibration profile. To provide hotkey service updates, we collect your device model and the installed hotkey service version to determine whether a new version is available.

(2) Meeting assistant

We provide a meeting assistant feature that supports meeting app management, auto framing, noise reduction and live captions. Live captions require you to sign in with a Xiaomi Account; we collect your Xiaomi Account ID, microphone sound and system audio and upload them to the server for speech-to-text conversion and to generate real-time captions. If you turn on translation, the captions will show the translation.

(3) Driver management

In "Driver management" you can scan, repair and update your device's drivers to keep your device running stably. For this purpose we collect your product serial number to find drivers that match your device.

(4) AI smart scheduling

We provide AI smart scheduling, which intelligently switches the performance state of the current device, including CPU/GPU state and memory state. For this purpose we collect screenshot information, device status information and the usage status of the camera, microphone and speakers. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(5) AI screen brightness adjustment

To provide smart screen brightness adjustment, we collect screenshot information. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(6) AI file search

To provide smart search for images, videos, audio and other files, we collect information about the files stored locally on your device (including file (folder) name, content, size, time, type, location and the name of the disk partition the file is on). Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(7) Inhouse installation guide

We provide the Inhouse installation guide, through which you can download software suited to the current device's system, such as Xiaomi PC Manager or Xiao AI. For this purpose we collect your device's unique identifier (SN), system version number and Xiaomi PC Manager version number.

(8) Performance modes

We provide performance modes, through which you can switch the performance mode of the current device automatically or manually. Adjustments include the Windows power mode, screen backlight brightness, keyboard backlight brightness, CPU/GPU state and memory state.

(9) Service and feedback

Through "Service and feedback" you can search FAQs, view Xiaomi's after-sales policy and service centers, submit feedback and contact online customer service.

1) When you search FAQs, we collect your search keywords to show you search results. Search keywords are not associated with your device or your Xiaomi Account. Please note that your search keywords alone cannot identify you and are not your personal information, and we have the right to use them; only when your search keywords are combined with your other information and can identify you will we, for the duration of such combined use, treat them as your personal information and process and protect them together with your search history in accordance with this privacy policy. Rest assured that we only provide search results based on your keywords and do not perform search result optimization.

2) If you run into a problem with your laptop, you can submit feedback or suggestions to us. To help us locate the problem, we need to collect your device information (including device model and name, operating system version and device status information), configuration choices, usage information of the device (and its related components) and product features, Xiaomi PC Manager version, feedback type, problem description, problem type and time of occurrence. To make it easier to locate the problem and reply to you in time, when submitting feedback you can choose to provide your contact details (mobile number or email address); we may use them to follow up with you and ask further questions about the problem.

Some problems require user logs for analysis and diagnosis. You can choose to tick "upload problem logs"; in this case, depending on the problem you report, the logs we capture differ, and so does the personal information they contain.

2. Device connectivity services
(1) Account sign-in

To connect and share between your Xiaomi PC and other devices, we collect your device identifier (SN) and Xiaomi Account information and use this information locally for security verification of device connectivity features. You can sign in to your Xiaomi Account by scanning a QR code, with your account, or by one-tap authorization from another signed-in app. After sign-in, your Xiaomi Account ID, nickname and avatar are shown on the PC Manager home page; this information is retrieved from the Xiaomi Account server and displayed.

(2) Cross-device connection and sharing

To connect and share between your Xiaomi PC and other Xiaomi devices such as phones, tablets, TVs and earphones, and to provide control and collaboration between devices, transfer of information and files, and cross-device screen mirroring and extension, we need to collect the device identifier (SN), device name, device ID, Xiaomi Account ID, device type, device model, IP address, Bluetooth MAC address, WLAN MAC address and WLAN SSID of the device you are using and of the device to be connected, in order to determine whether pairing is supported, to establish near-field connections and to record feature usage. Rest assured that this information is used only to make these determinations and provide the related features, and not for any other purpose.

This service transfers data between devices via Bluetooth or WLAN. Except for the device ID, Xiaomi Account ID, device type, device name, device identifier (SN), IP address and the hashed WLAN SSID, which are uploaded to the server, no other data is uploaded.

(3) Device center

To make device connectivity services easier to use, we provide a device control entry point: in the device center you can see nearby devices and operate them. To display phones, tablets, TVs and Bluetooth earphones, we may need to collect your device type and device name to synchronize and display the related device information.

(4) App continuity

We provide app continuity, which lets apps move between and be used on different devices. To implement this feature we may collect information about the app you are using (including package name, name and icon) to show the app continuity notification; you can tap the notification shown on the other device to open the app to continue.

(5) Cross-device information transfer

We provide cross-device notifications and a cross-device clipboard. With cross-device notifications you can receive notifications from your phone on your laptop. With the cross-device clipboard you can copy text, images and files and paste them on other devices signed in to the same Xiaomi Account; to implement this feature we may need to collect the text, images and files you copy in order to transfer them. Rest assured that we do not actually read or store the content of this information.

(6) Screen mirroring and extension

We provide screen mirroring and extension, through which you can mirror the device screen or a window to another tablet or TV, or use another tablet or TV as an extended display for this device.

(7) Earphone connection and control

We provide earphone connection and control, through which you can quickly connect and control earphones on your PC. For this purpose we collect your Xiaomi Account and PC model.

(8) Hardware collaboration

We provide hardware collaboration across multiple devices. With keyboard and mouse collaboration you can share your laptop's keyboard and mouse with a tablet for input control and drag files across devices; for this purpose we collect your files and keyboard and mouse key events. With camera collaboration you can use another device's camera for recording, taking photos and preview; for this purpose we collect the camera video streams of this device and the other devices. With network collaboration you can turn on and connect to your phone's hotspot with one tap. With call collaboration you can receive calls from a phone on the same local network on your laptop; for this purpose we collect your microphone audio stream. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(9) Controlling the PC with wearables

We provide control of the PC with wearable devices, through which you can use a wearable to turn pages on your PC. For this purpose we collect your wearable's key events. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(10) Xiaomi Audio Relay

We provide cross-device audio transfer, which lets the audio content on your device be cast directly to a PC on the same local network for playback, with seamless switching of audio between devices. For this purpose we collect your Xiaomi Account, device identifiers (device ID, SN), device name, device model, WLAN MAC address and system volume. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(11) Mi Share

We provide Mi Share, through which you can send files on your device to other smart devices nearby. For this purpose we collect your PC's device name, manufacturer, WLAN SSID, WLAN channel, file names and file content. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(12) Tap to transfer

We provide tap to transfer: after you tap your phone on the PC's NFC area, files are transferred quickly. For this purpose we collect your Bluetooth address and NFC tag content. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(13) Cross-device files

We provide cross-device files, through which you can search, access, open or edit, on the current device, files on other smart devices (phones, tablets, etc.) signed in to the same Xiaomi Account. For this purpose we collect your device type, device name, product type, IP address, device MAC address and file information (including file name and file content). To ensure secure transfer of your files we perform cross-device identity authentication; for this purpose we collect your service ID (calculated from the device ID, the device's unique FID and the Xiaomi Account). Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

(14) Phone desktop sharing

We provide phone desktop sharing, through which you can open your phone's desktop on your laptop and operate the device screen. For this purpose we collect your files and keyboard and mouse key events. Rest assured that this information is processed only locally and is not uploaded to the cloud.

3. Control Center
To make Xiaomi PC Manager features easier to use, we provide a central control entry point: in the "Control Center" you can directly choose on-device services, or see nearby devices and control and collaborate with them. After you agree to our privacy policy, some of the services above start automatically at boot so that we can provide you with more efficient and smoother service.
